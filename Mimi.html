Our Manufacturing Pillars

From raw material sourcing to automated clean-room processing, explore how Mimi sets the global standard for feline safety and luxury.

Precision Woodworking & Scratch Posts

We utilize sustainably harvested Nordic Beechwood and Baltic Birch. Scratching columns feature zero-VOC natural jute and high-density hand-wrapped sisal fibers, tested against high-tension clawing without shredding or chemical residue.

  • Non-toxic plant adhesives
  • Heavy weighted anti-topple bases

Gourmet Organic Culinary Processing

Our human-grade processing facility utilizes sub-zero freeze-drying technology to preserve 98% of natural micronutrients. Free of synthetic preservatives, grain fillers, or artificial dyes, formulated under strict veterinary oversight.

  • Human-grade protein sourcing
  • Pathogen-free cleanroom freeze-drying

Smart Feline Tech & IoT Systems

Engineered with ultra-quiet brushless motors operating below 25dB. Smart water fountains feature triple-stage carbon filtration, and automated litter enclosures feature multi-point safety infrared sensors.

  • Whisper-quiet acoustic dampening
  • Mobile app integration & health metrics
